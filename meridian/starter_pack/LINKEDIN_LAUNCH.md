# LinkedIn launch draft

I’ve been developing **Meridian Intelligence**, an AI-assisted product concept focused on a problem that interests me: the gap between compliance screening and deeper corporate intelligence research.

The idea is one platform with two connected workspaces:

- **Risk & Compliance** for screening review, potential matches, and case management.
- **Intelligence & Investigations** for entity research, relationship mapping, evidence tracking, and source-backed briefings.

The key design decision is a shared intelligence layer. Analysts should be able to move from a potential screening concern into a deeper investigation without rebuilding the entity record or losing the source trail.

I’m approaching this as an analyst-support tool—not an automated decision-maker. The product concept emphasizes provenance, uncertainty labels, explainable potential matches, human review, and auditability.

The current prototype uses fictional demo data. My next milestones are persistence, source integrations, a carefully evaluated matching workflow, and AI-generated summaries grounded in traceable sources.

I’m building this to keep learning by doing across AI product design, international trade risk, compliance, and corporate intelligence. Feedback from people working in these areas is welcome.

## Portfolio case-study outline

1. **Problem:** screening tools and investigative research workflows can fragment entity context and evidence.
2. **User:** analyst handling a potential match or counterparty concern.
3. **Design decision:** two workspaces, one shared entity/source/case layer.
4. **Trust model:** provenance, confidence vs. verification status, explicit unknowns, human-reviewed disposition.
5. **Prototype:** mode switch, entity profile, screening review, relationship graph, report preview.
6. **Next validation:** interview 5–8 practitioners; test whether cross-workspace handoff reduces re-entry and improves source traceability.
7. **Evaluation:** task completion time, sourced-claim coverage, false positives on labelled test cases, and analyst correction rate for AI output.

Do not describe the prototype as production-ready or claim live sanctions coverage until implemented and tested.
